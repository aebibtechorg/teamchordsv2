Most music teams do not struggle because they lack talent. They struggle because the workflow is messy.

Songs live in too many places. Setlists change in too many chats. Projection needs one version, rehearsals need another, and live service needs everything to stay calm when the tempo rises.

We built Team Chords to reduce that friction. The goal is simple: keep the music moving and the team aligned.

That means a better way to organize chord sheets, a cleaner way to assemble setlists, and a live output flow that does not turn into a last-minute fire drill.

Team Chords is not just a repository of songs. It is a system for the real pace of team music.
