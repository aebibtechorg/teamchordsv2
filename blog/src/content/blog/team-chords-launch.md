Team Chords is here.

We built it for the moment every team knows: the room is full, the rehearsal got intense, the setlist changed, and you still need a calm, reliable way to keep everyone on the same page.

With Team Chords, chord sheets, setlists, and live outputs finally live in one place. That means less switching, less scrambling, and fewer "which version are we on?" moments right before you play.

This launch is the start of something bigger. We’re building a home for teams that need to move fast without losing the music.

If you’ve ever juggled songs, set order, and projection at the same time, this was built for you.
