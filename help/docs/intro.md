# Welcome to Team Chords Help

Team Chords helps your team manage chord sheets, build set lists, and share live-ready song views from a single workspace.

This help center is organized by feature so you can quickly find the workflow you need:

- **Getting started** with your account and organization
- **Library** management for chord sheets
- **Chord sheet editing** with ChordPro and format conversion
- **Set lists** for rehearsals, services, and performances
- **Outputs / live display** for printable and shareable set list views
- **Team management** for invites and member roles
- **Billing** for upgrades, billing portal access, and cancellations
- **Profile & authentication** for account settings and onboarding tours
- **Troubleshooting** for common issues

## What Team Chords is built for

Team Chords is especially useful for:

- worship teams
- gigging bands
- music directors
- collaborative song libraries
- teams that need shared, transposed, live-ready charts

## How the app is organized

After signing in, most teams spend their time in these areas:

1. **Library** — store and search chord sheets
2. **Set Lists** — organize songs into a sequence
3. **Team** — invite and manage members
4. **Billing** — manage the organization’s plan
5. **Profile** — update your account and musician preferences

## Recommended first steps

If you're new to Team Chords, follow this order:

1. Create or join an organization
2. Add a few chord sheets to the library
3. Build your first set list
4. Share or preview the set list
5. Invite the rest of your team
