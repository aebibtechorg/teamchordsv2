# Profile & Authentication

Your Profile page combines personal account details with musician-specific preferences.

## What you can manage

From Profile, you can update:

- first name
- last name
- musical role
- instruments
- preferred key
- website
- bio

You can also:

- replay onboarding tours
- open the password update flow

## Account identity

Team Chords shows your:

- avatar
- email address
- active organization
- role in the active organization

This helps confirm which team context you are currently using.

## Musician details

The musician section is designed to store personal preferences and profile metadata.

Examples include:

- role on the team
- instruments you play
- preferred key
- personal website
- short biography

## Saving profile changes

When you save changes, Team Chords updates:

1. your core user profile
2. your musician profile details

After saving, the app refreshes your profile data.

## Replaying tours

If you want another walkthrough of the app, the Profile page can replay:

- the Library tour
- the Set Lists tour

This is especially useful when:

- you skipped onboarding the first time
- the UI has changed
- you are training a teammate beside you

## Updating your password

The Profile page includes an **Update Password** option.

Use it if you need to:

- rotate credentials
- strengthen account security
- recover from an old password you no longer want to use

## Auth and sign-in notes

Team Chords uses Auth0-based authentication.

That means your access experience may include:

- redirect-based login
- callback handling
- invite-aware sign-up/sign-in flows

## Organization context

Your visible organization and role come from the organizations attached to your account profile.

If something looks wrong, check whether:

- you joined the correct organization
- your invite was accepted correctly
- your account needs to be refreshed after a recent change
